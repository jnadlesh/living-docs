# Plans

The plans that issues were drawn from. A plan is written when a body of work needs thinking through before it becomes issues: the design, the order, the risks. Once its issues exist, the issues are the tracker and the plan is background.

- Name a plan after its subject and the day it was written, such as `sync-2026-09-17.md`.
- A plan links the issues it became, and each issue links back to the plan under Where.
- When the work lands and the pages in `docs/` say what is true, the plan moves to `archive/`.
