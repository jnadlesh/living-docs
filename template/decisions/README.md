# Decisions

Why things are the way they are. One file per decision, numbered in the order they were made.

- A decision says what was chosen, what it was chosen over, and what would reverse it. A decision without an alternative is a preference. A decision without a reversal condition is dogma.
- Each carries a status line under its title: `proposed`, `accepted`, `accepted, amended by` a later decision, `superseded by` a later decision, or `withdrawn`.
- A decision is never rewritten to say something new. A new decision supersedes it, and only the status line of the old one changes.
- Pages in `docs/` link here from their Decisions heading. Pages say what is true. Decisions say why.
- A new decision takes the next free number. Name the file `NNNN-short-title.md` and start its title with the number, like `# 0001 — Short title`.
